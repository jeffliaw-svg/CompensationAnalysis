Methodology & Sources

Overview

This analysis benchmarks the competitive wage environment for Copart operations roles against four comparable employers (Walmart, Home Depot, Costco, and Starbucks) across all 197 Copart US locations. Facilities are ranked by blended competitive wage and divided into quartiles (Q1 highest to Q4 lowest).

Quartile Methodology

Facilities are ranked by blended competitive wage, computed as an inverse-distance weighted average of employer wages within 25 miles:

Blended = Σ(wagei / disti) / Σ(1 / disti)

Competitors within 25 miles are weighted by 1/distance. Competitors beyond 25 miles are excluded (N/A). The 197 locations are divided into quartiles: Q1 (top 25%, highest competitive wages) through Q4 (bottom 25%). Q1 locations face the most competitive hiring environment.

Distance Estimates & Cutoff

Hover any employer wage cell to see estimated distance to nearest store. If beyond 25 miles, it is marked N/A and excluded from blended score. Distances are estimated from metro classification and store density.
